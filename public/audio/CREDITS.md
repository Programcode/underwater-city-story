# Audio credits

The voice lines, mood beds, and sound effects in this directory were generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io): narration and character speech with the `eleven_v3` text-to-speech model, and the music and effects with the ElevenLabs sound-generation API. `manifest.json` records the render (`generatedAt`: 2026-06-09).

Project notes describe this set as free-plan output. ElevenLabs does not grant a commercial license for audio made on the free plan or while signed out. Publishing that audio requires attribution: the title of the published work includes `elevenlabs.io` or `11.ai`. This repo shows that credit on the title screen and in the README. If these clips were made on the free plan, add the same credit to the public title (repository description or page title) before treating the release as fully attributed.

Confirm the plan that produced these files before any commercial use. Audio generated during a paid ElevenLabs subscription can carry a commercial license under ElevenLabs' terms; free-plan audio cannot, and it is not relicensed by the MIT license on the source code.
